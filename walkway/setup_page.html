Walkway zone setup

Pink dot = someone standing (with a line showing where they're heading), blue ring = someone seated. A yellow zone is armed: someone just stood up next to it, so it will react on the first step in. Screen is at the top. Brighter means taller. Drag on empty space to draw a zone, drag inside a zone to move it, and drag its corner to resize it. Changes take effect right away. Press Save to keep them after a restart.
